HOW TO PLAY

THE GOAL

Clear the board. Every tile has exactly one twin with the same shape and colour. Make each pair meet so both vanish.

MOVING

Swipe (or use arrow keys / WASD). Every tile on the board moves one step in that direction. Tiles in a line move together. Walls, dark obstacles and tiles that cannot move block the tiles pushing into them. A swipe that moves nothing is ignored: it costs no move.

MATCHING

A match happens when a tile is pushed into a stopped tile of the same shape and colour. Both vanish. Tiles travelling side by side never match, so one of them must be stopped by a wall, an obstacle or another tile. Different tiles never vanish; they only block each other.

ONE-WAY GATES (LEVEL 16+)

A cell with an arrow is a one-way gate. A tile can only enter it by moving in the arrow direction. From any other side it blocks like a wall, and the echo obeys the same rule. A tile standing on a gate can leave in any direction. Gates never move, and two tiles do not match through a closed gate side.

THE ECHO

After every move, your previous move repeats by itself. The first move of a level has no echo. The ECHO arrow at the top shows which direction will repeat after your next move. It is always exact, never random. The echo is free: only your own swipes count as moves. If your move clears the board, no echo follows.

STARS ★

Every level has a PAR: the fewest moves it can be solved in, found by checking every possibility. ★★★ finish in par moves or fewer. ★★ finish within 2 moves of par. ★ finish at all. Your best score is saved, and a level unlocks when you earn at least one star on the one before it. Every level can be completed for free.

STUCK?

If no solution remains, the game tells you. Restart is one tap and instant, and the Echo resets with it. Mistakes are part of the puzzle: plan one move ahead, and remember what the echo will do.

SHAPES

● circle, ■ square, ▲ triangle. Each colour also has its own shape, so you never need to tell colours apart.
